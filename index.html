Architectural Specification · Research Reference

Legal Eye: Double-Checking Transactions in Irrevocable Payment Networks

Full Stack Development · CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Bangalore

With the worldwide expansion of real-time payment networks—such as India's Unified Payments Interface (UPI) and the United States' FedNow—transactions settle within seconds. While this velocity provides instant convenience, it creates a serious systemic challenge: once money moves, it is permanently gone.

1. Why Outdated Rule Engines Fail

Traditional banking systems rely on rigid, fixed rules (for example, flagging any payment where amount > ₹50,000). In the modern world, this approach breaks down:

  • Blocking Honest Customers: Over 80% of halted transactions are actually legitimate cardholders, causing frustration and brand abandonment.
  • Organized Fraud: Scammers easily bypass fixed limits by splitting stolen money into smaller payments just below the trigger amount.
"A fraud prevention model that cannot explain itself in plain language is practically unusable. When an artificial intelligence blocks a user's transfer without a human-understandable reason, it introduces immediate customer friction and legal risk."

2. The Legal Eye Two-Stage Solution

Legal Eye solves this dilemma by dividing transaction verification into two synchronized stages:

  1. Instant Pre-Settlement Double-Check (< 16ms): Evaluates device fingerprints, travel speed, and transaction amounts directly inside the payment path.
  2. Automatic Plain-Language Explanations: Whenever an anomaly is detected, Legal Eye generates an immediate plain-English summary explaining exactly why the transfer was paused.
